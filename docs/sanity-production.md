# Production Sanity

Production Sanity is authorized October 5. The normal `/profile [player]` now reads the selected player's saved Sanity and renders the existing background eye and attached balance/status. No testing subcommands are added.

Start and cap are 100. First qualifying activity per Pacific day earns +2; a second qualifying message at least 30 minutes later earns +1, up to +3/day. Each completed inactive Pacific day loses 1, settled at startup, 00:05 Pacific and profile/activity reads. Existing shared-channel eligibility is retained separately from personal XP. Actual bots and production Bots-role members are excluded. Community XP remains paused, with no Community tables, awards or worker introduced.

At 69 or below, the next qualifying post may send a private DM, reserved before that post earns presence. A rolling seven-day cooldown prevents repeated messages; zero has one special lifetime notification under the same cooldown. Closed or uncertain DMs consume the reservation, never generate a public fallback and never prevent activity credit. Player copy invites community participation without publishing recovery formulas.

The independent production runtime receives only Sanity services, its configuration, pure shared eligibility/date helpers, profile service, a minimal message-hook patch and the daily worker hook. Existing Spooky purchase rules, command registry, Fate/Bank, personal XP, birthday rewards and level-up messages are preserved. Existing development accounts and history are preserved.

Tracking activation and event spending activation are separate switches. `productionEnabled` enables tracking/rendering; `productionSpendingEnabled` remains false while development spending stays enabled. This prevents future command generation from silently changing the existing production Spooky purchase contract during this profile rollout.

Deployment evidence, verified stopped-writer backups, source rollback files and actual-production memory-only PNG probes are retained under `.runtime/sanity-production`. Production receives only the four additive Sanity tables and full-start accounts for current eligible human members, with durable baseline receipts and no backdated inactivity. All preexisting table contents must match before and after the migration. New human arrivals initialize at 100 when first viewed or active.

Live rollout completed October 5: fresh paginated REST roster found 44 eligible humans and excluded 5 bot/Bots-role members. All 44 accounts and daily states were initialized at 100 with baseline receipts. Verified stopped-writer backups passed integrity and foreign-key checks; all 20 preexisting production tables retained identical contents and the development database remained byte-identical through deployment. No real DM or public profile was sent during rollout.

Validation: final full suite 513/513 and focused production/eligibility/reminder checks 12/12 pass; offline registry audit reports no command changes.
