## Confirmed rules and constraints

- Candy starts at 10 on first economic touch, with no catch-up. Refill +10 each three elapsed hours, capacity 80. Registration reuses a materialized nonparticipant row. Below cap retain partial intervals; discard surplus at cap before spending. Lazy accrual makes a periodic candy worker unnecessary for correctness. No daily action ceiling.
- Every trick/treat costs exactly one candy, including break-curse and cursed distributions. Gifts mint only actually delivered capped candy, with no extra caller charge.
- Treat probabilities: lost 20%, Sweet Tooth 5%, double gift 15%, immunity 10%, break curse 5%, standard gift 32%, Eye 13%. Trick: steal 25%, heist 10%, reverse nickname 10%, curse 5%, backfire 5%, caught 32%, Eye 13%. Eyes are outcomes, never independent bonus rolls.
- Cursed callers have a 10% replacement chance. Cursed treats spread curse 20% or distribute to up to three 80%; cursed tricks distribute to up to two. Effective normal Eye chance 11.7% is approved. Message transformation is 20%, reverse/shuffle equally.
- Candy theft can target eligible server humans, including nonregistered members/admins. Eye theft targets funded registered players; transfer one Eye, or find one only when no eligible funded target exists. Permission/database failures never mint fallback Eyes. No quarter theft, recency restriction or daily Eye-loss cap.
- Giver and recipient receive one-hour theft protection. Curse and reversed nickname last until broken or October end. Preserve the first original nickname across repeat hits; restore only when the current nickname still equals the bot-applied value. Remove only bot-owned curse roles.
- Every five Eyes automatically awards an ordinary quarter in the same transaction. No hard four-draw/day limit: four/20 is an aspiration, not a guarantee. Ten banked fate buys another quarter, unlimited affordable draws for any registered bank holder; never spend unbanked fate.
- Ordinary rarity is approved **70% common / 22% rare / 8% legendary**, uniform within rarity. Characters `had/hfm/mrq/max/nik/qam/sel`; stable position IDs `tl/tr/bl/br`: blue common tops, purple rare bottom-left, gold legendary bottom-right.
- Every five extras across arbitrary pieces automatically consumes exactly five and grants a uniformly random missing piece, regardless of rarity. First copies survive. Repeat batches; full collections retain extras.
- Four pieces complete a character and atomically award its permanent badge once. /badges view/leaderboard, profiles and level-ups share rendering; Selene resolves the named server emoji, other earned badges use medals, unowned badges use question marks. Seven full circles and 105 token subsets are shipped; six badge artworks and live emoji/access verification remain pending.
- Sweet Tooth retains the legacy random role. Already-owning callers trigger a random eligible other recipient and capped +5 candy generosity. Its normal treat also gives an Unwanted caller +1 bank, capped at 100; no independent fate roll.
- Prestige scoring v1: normal success +2, lost/caught −1, curse replacement +1, other paid no-effect 0. Separate tracks; actual actors qualify, admins eligible, tied leaders share titles, one player may win both. Exact weights stay out of player-facing embeds. Final title names/role IDs remain undecided; Sweet Tooth is the random gameplay role.
- Other-player events are public and tag registered targets only. Nonregistered names remain plain text with restricted allowed mentions. Personal screens/results are ephemeral. Quarter and newly completed-character announcements are large/bold and public.
- Active interval: `2026-10-01T07:00:00Z` inclusive to `2026-11-01T07:00:00Z` exclusive (`America/Los_Angeles`). No post-October redemption; modeled REDEMPTION has zero duration. No newcomer catch-up.

## Architecture decisions to preserve

1. `config/runtime.js` alone selects credentials/storage. Development requires `.env.development`; production explicit `.env.production`; no generic `.env` or inherited credential fallback. Tests inject disposable connections. Never print tokens.
2. Use `createEconomy.execute` as the root mutation. Construct with `configVersion: event.version` (4); its historical default 1 is unsuitable for live entries. All related writes use `ctx.transaction`; no nested economy calls or Discord calls inside it.
3. Interaction identity is globally unique; replay checks actor/scope/type and returns the durable receipt without rerolling. Worker keys are event/guild scoped. Identity does not fingerprint every arbitrary payload, so admin interfaces must define stable operation semantics.
4. The economy uses IMMEDIATE SQLite transactions and a shared per-connection queue. Constraints enforce nonnegative integer resources, candy ≤80, positive inventory and scoped uniqueness. Theft debit/credit is atomic and equal.
5. All registered Eye credits, including transfers/admin corrections, must route through `collection.creditEyes`; raw balance helpers do not perform conversion. Acquisition/exchange/eligibility share the root transaction.
6. Receipt finalization stores exact rendering/mention decisions. Public notification enqueue occurs before commit; external sends occur afterwards. Never refund or re-award because a send failed.
7. Role/nickname Delivery tracks latest desired state/revision and reconciles fresh Discord state after commit. Nickname conflicts remain explicit. Cleanup must persist restoration intent before removing effects and precede reconciliation.
8. Notification states are `pending/sending/sent/uncertain/cancelled`; reset cancellation is audited and terminal. Delivery also supports cancelled intents. `notifications.deliver` returns allSent confirmation; cursed originals are deleted only when true. Claim before external send; record successful message ID; use short hashed enforced nonce. Ambiguous failure or crash after claim is not blindly retried. This prevents automatic repeated attempts, but cannot guarantee delivery. Discord nonce deduplication alone is short-lived.
9. Runtime requires correct guild/channel, complete trusted member snapshots and role/hierarchy permissions before mutations. Registration creates the associated User account transactionally. Single-process queues do not provide distributed leases.
10. Fate wallet CAS rejects stale whole-wallet saves; atomic SQL credits preserve current bank values. This protects bank purchases from covered legacy writers without making all legacy rewards globally idempotent.
